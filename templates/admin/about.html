About Sahayak

Sahayak is a comprehensive service marketplace platform that connects customers with verified service providers. This admin panel allows administrators to manage all aspects of the platform including users, providers, bookings, and services.

Key Features

  • ✅ User Management - Manage customers and service providers
  • ✅ Provider Verification - Approve or reject provider applications
  • ✅ Booking Management - Monitor and manage all service bookings
  • ✅ Service Categories - Add, edit, and manage service categories
  • ✅ Reviews & Ratings - View and moderate customer reviews
  • ✅ Analytics Dashboard - Track platform statistics and metrics
